## Email is a flat card timeline; the bubbles and the thread list both retire (2026-09-01)

Ported from Nudge the day it shipped there (Serdar: "apply the same to randevu
app too"). The email tab has now been three things: Gmail-style flush thread
rows, then bubbles in chat order (2026-08-17 — "everything you can open in
this pane is a bubble"), and now one CARD per email, newest first, on the chat
paper without its doodles (`.chat-wallpaper.chat-plain`, a modifier so the
dark tokens keep scoping). **This supersedes the 2026-08-17 bubbles decision**;
what the card model fixes over both predecessors is that the newest mail sits
at the top with its body already showing, and there is nothing to navigate
into at all.

- **The subject-grouped thread UI is deleted, not restyled.** `openThread`,
  `threadKeyOf` and the `emailThreads` reducer are gone. Grouping by
  normalized subject was a display guess (threading needs Message-ID/
  References headers, which nothing stores); a CRM timeline's job is to be a
  trustworthy log, and a wrong grouping is worse than no grouping — HubSpot
  never threads for the same reason. `normalizeSubject` keeps its REAL job
  untouched: binding inbound mail to a customer, server-side.
- **The card**: full subject wrapping (truncation is the HubSpot defect the
  design fixes), customer's name emerald on their mail / "You" on ours,
  time-only stamp under day PILLS (WhatsApp's own date chip), body behind a
  MEASURED 240px clamp (`ClampedEmailContent` — scrollHeight vs the clamp,
  re-checked by ResizeObserver as images decode and the quote-collapse opens;
  never guessed from length, so short emails get no dead Show-more). The
  quote-collapse `•••` (`QuotedEmailBody`/`QuotedPlainBody`) rides inside the
  card and pairs naturally with per-email cards: each card shows only its new
  content. The clamp's fade matches what it covers — `from-white` over the
  email-paper, `from-[var(--chat-field)]` over plain text on the card.
- **Reply is per card** and prefills `Re: ${stripReplyPrefixes(subject)}` —
  that Re: is for the CUSTOMER's inbox; ours no longer threads. The header
  action is always Compose. Reply hides on failed/queued/parked sends; a
  parked send shows its stamp + Cancel in the card footer instead.
- **Routing is a scroll, not a navigation**: the All index and the deep link
  set `focusEmailId`, the tab renders, an rAF scrolls to `[data-email-id]`.
  The card list reads `focusId` once at mount so its first slab (20 cards,
  IntersectionObserver sentinel 600px early) covers a routed card deeper than
  the slab — scrollIntoView on an unmounted card is a silent no-op.
- The bubble loop no longer has email branches (`emailHtml`/`QuotedPlainBody`
  there were dead the moment email stopped reaching it); `--thread-bg` as an
  email surface went with them.
- Verified in the browser on Bella: cards, date pills, `•••`, Reply prefill
  (single Re:), Compose from the header. i18n grew `email.showMore`/
  `email.showLess` (tr/en/de).

## Calls take the card treatment too (2026-09-01)

Same-day follow-up to the email cards, again ported from Nudge on Serdar's
ask ("apply the call cards to randevu too"). The call tab's flush rows and
the open-call detail navigation are both gone: one CARD per call, newest
first, on the plain paper (`chat-plain`), day pills saying the date, each
card carrying direction + duration + time, the recording player
(`preload="none"` — a history of recordings must not fetch every stream on
tab entry), the summary, and the ended-line. **Details** unfolds the
collected values + transcript IN the card; a deep link / All-index route
scrolls to the card and pre-expands it (`focusCallId`, read once at
CallCards' mount).

- **The "no outcome pill" decision (2026-08-25) stands** — the summary is the
  outcome; the card header says only direction and duration.
- **The transcript sits straight on the card with the CALLER bubble retinted
  grey** (`[--chat-field:#f0f0f0]` / dark `#3a3a3a` on a wrapper). Its bubble
  is `--chat-field`, invisible on this `--chat-field` card; Nudge tried a
  paper inset first and Serdar rejected it on sight — it matched the page
  ground exactly and read as a hole punched through the card. The var
  override keeps `CallTranscript` untouched for Voice → Calls.
- **Deleted**: `openCallId`, the back-arrow header, the call index rows, and
  `CallEntry` whole (its non-detail form was already unreachable — the
  bubble map only ever saw calls via the open-call path). A
  `channel === "call"` early-return SURVIVES in the bubble map purely to
  narrow the union for the code below it; removing it is a type error, not a
  cleanup. `CollectedList` is exported from CallDetail.tsx now; `CallDetail`
  itself remains for Voice → Calls.
- i18n grew `calls.details` / `calls.hideDetails` (tr/en/de).
- Verified on Bella: four inbound-call cards with Turkish summaries, Details
  unfolding Gender + the transcript (grey caller, green agent), day pills.

## A Cloud row's bubble now shows what the phone received (2026-09-01)

Serdar, reading a confirmation bubble in the thread: "why do i see the url
and this text — i don't see that stuff in whatsapp." He was right both ways:
the app's bubble carried the RICH body ([pricelist] bullets and the
[manage_link] paragraph) while the phone got the registered Cloud text, which
deliberately drops both (lib/metaTemplate: the platform-owned
CLOUD_TEMPLATE_TEXTS carry no link — the confirm/cancel buttons are the
manage link's job, and UNSENDABLE tokens can't register anyway). The
DIVERGENCE is by design; STAMPING the rich body on the Cloud row was the bug
— the thread showed a URL and prices the customer never received, and the
recent template-bubble work ("show the buttons the customer saw") had fixed
only the button half of that honesty.

- `sendAppointmentMessage` now stamps `deliveredBody` on Cloud rows: the
  registered body filled with the very `bodyParams` the send carries.
  `submittedBody` (named tokens, metaParams order) is the primary source;
  `bodyPreview` ({{1}}/{{2}}/{{3}}, hand-registered rows) the fallback —
  whose positional triple matches the fallback bodyParams by construction —
  and a row with neither keeps today's rich body rather than going blank.
  The demo branch stamps the same thing ("its rows carry what a real send
  would have"). Evolution and email keep the rich body untouched — those
  lanes really do send it.
- Same frozen-snapshot rule as Nudge's wa_cloud lane ("the timeline never
  lies about what went out", 2026-08-07 there).
- FORWARD-ONLY: rows already written keep the rich body. They carry
  templateName + templateParams, so a backfill is possible if the lie in old
  history ever matters; not built unasked.
